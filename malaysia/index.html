FAQ

What are TikTok Shop Malaysia's seller fees in 2026?

On a normal delivered order you pay a commission fee by product category (different for Marketplace and Mall, and lower if you join the Bonus Extra Program), a 3.78% transaction fee (4.86% on TikTok PayLater orders from 16 October 2026), and a flat RM0.54 Platform Support Fee per order. BXP sellers also pay a 4.86% BXP service fee, or 7.02% on campaign days. All rates include 8% SST.

What changes on 31 October 2026?

TikTok Shop Malaysia revises Marketplace and Mall commission rates for orders placed from 00:00 on 31 October 2026, Malaysia time. Orders placed before that date follow the old rates. Most categories go up (for example non-BXP Marketplace skincare moves from 15.12% to 16.74%), while some essentials such as feminine care, medical supplies, OTC medicine and nursing & feeding go down, and fresh food, baking, dairy and staples stay at 0%.

How is the TikTok Shop commission fee calculated in Malaysia?

Commission = (item price − seller discount) × the category rate. Shipping and TikTok platform discounts are not part of the base. The published rates already include SST.

How is the transaction fee calculated?

Since 15 February 2026 the transaction fee is (original item price − seller discount + shipping paid by the buyer) × 3.78%. From 16 October 2026 orders paid with TikTok PayLater are charged 4.86% instead. Platform discounts are not deducted from the base.

What is the RM0.54 Platform Support Fee?

A flat fee of RM0.50 plus 8% SST (RM0.54) per successfully delivered order, regardless of order value or quantity, introduced on 15 February 2026. It is not refunded if a delivered order is later returned. Some categories and eligible new or small sellers can get it rebated or waived.

Is joining BXP worth it after the new rates?

From 31 October 2026, BXP lowers your commission by roughly 2.2 to 6 percentage points depending on category (2.70 points for many categories, such as skincare), but adds a 4.86% BXP service fee (7.02% on campaign days, capped at RM54 per item) on every completed order. Tick and untick 'Bonus Extra Program' in the calculator to compare your own numbers.

Are TikTok Shop Malaysia fees inclusive of SST?

Yes. TikTok publishes the commission, transaction, BXP and Platform Support Fee rates inclusive of 8% SST, and this calculator uses those SST-inclusive figures.
