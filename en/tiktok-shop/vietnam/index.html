    How TikTok Shop Vietnam fees work

    TikTok Shop Vietnam charges three fees on a normal delivered order. TikTok’s own fees already include Vietnamese tax for sellers without a verified company tax code.

    1. Platform commission fee

    The commission depends on the category and on whether you sell as a Marketplace or a Mall shop. Marketplace rates are from the table effective 3 July 2026; Mall rates are from the table effective 20 October 2026, which also cut Mall book categories from 15% to 12.5%. The table has more than 2,000 categories, from 1.5% to 16% for Marketplace and 1.5% to 19.8% for Mall. Categories not in the table pay the default 14% (Marketplace) or 16% (Mall). Commission is charged on the item price after seller discounts. Shipping is not included.

    2. Transaction fee

    6% for orders created from 9 May 2026, on the amount the customer paid plus any platform discount, less refunds. In practice that is the item price after seller discounts plus the shipping the buyer paid. From 21 October 2026, shops that spend on ads get a lower transaction fee, based on their ads take rate over the last 30 days: 0.5 percentage points off at 4% to under 7%, 0.7 at 7% to under 10%, 1.0 at 10% to under 15%, 1.2 at 15% to under 20%, and 1.5 at 20% or more.

    3. Order Processing Fee

    3,000 ₫ per delivered order, since 27 October 2025.

    4. Tax withheld for individual and household sellers

    Since 1 July 2025, TikTok Shop withholds 1% VAT and 0.5% personal income tax from individual and business household sellers, on the item price after seller discount. Tick the option in the calculator if this applies to you. Registered companies declare their own tax instead.

    What is not included

    From 12 October 2026 there is also a logistics service fee for parcels over 10 kg, from 3,000 ₫ and capped at 65,000 ₫ per parcel. Affiliate commission and ads are optional. Add affiliate commission in its field, and any other cost under other costs.

    Worked examples

    Example 1: 199,000 ₫ serum, Marketplace shop

    Serums & Essences at 15.5%, product cost 60,000 ₫, free shipping for the buyer.

    Commission fee (15.5%)−30,845 ₫
    Transaction fee (6%)−11,940 ₫
    Order Processing Fee−3,000 ₫
    Total fees45,785 ₫
    Payout153,215 ₫

    Profit 93,215 ₫ (46.8% margin).

    Example 2: the same serum in a Mall shop

    The Mall rate is 18.8%: total fees 52,352 ₫, profit 86,648 ₫.

    Example 3: with a 10% ads take rate

    A Marketplace shop whose ads take rate is between 10% and 15% gets 1.0 point off the transaction fee from 21 October 2026. The fee falls to 5% (9,950 ₫) and total fees to 43,795 ₫.

    Example 4: individual seller

    As an individual or household seller, 2,985 ₫ of tax is also withheld, so the payout is 150,230 ₫.

    Example 5: 5,990,000 ₫ mobile phone

    Mobile phones pay 2% on Marketplace. Fees: 482,200 ₫, profit 207,800 ₫ on a 5,300,000 ₫ cost.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is the TikTok Shop Vietnam commission?

    It depends on the category. Marketplace rates run from 1.5% to 16% and Mall rates from 1.5% to 19.8%. Categories that are not listed pay 14% (Marketplace) or 16% (Mall).

    What is the TikTok Shop Vietnam transaction fee?

    6% from 9 May 2026, on the item price after seller discounts plus the shipping the buyer paid. From 21 October 2026, shops that spend on ads can get 0.5 to 1.5 points off.

    Is there a fixed fee per order?

    Yes. The Order Processing Fee is 3,000 ₫ per delivered order.

    Does TikTok Shop withhold tax in Vietnam?

    Yes. Since 1 July 2025 it withholds 1% VAT and 0.5% personal income tax from individual and business household sellers.
