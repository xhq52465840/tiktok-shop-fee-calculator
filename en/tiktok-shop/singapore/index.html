    How TikTok Shop Singapore fees work

    TikTok Shop Singapore's own fee overview lists two seller fees on a delivered order: a Platform Commission Fee and a Transaction Fee. Sellers in the Bonus Extra Program (BXP) pay a third, the BXP service fee. Every rate TikTok publishes for Singapore already includes 9% GST, so nothing is added on top.

    1. Platform Commission Fee

    From 00:00 on 1 September 2026 (GMT+8), the official table charges 9.810% to standard sellers and 7.085% to BXP sellers in every category. Rows in the "EL, Toys & Collectibles" industry — electronics, appliances, computers, toys, collectibles, vouchers and similar — carry a S$30 fee cap; every other row has no cap. The base is (item price − seller discount) × rate. Shipping and TikTok-funded discounts are not part of it.

    2. Transaction Fee

    3.27% (GST-inclusive) since 1 October 2025, up from 2.18%. Since 1 April 2026 it is charged on what the customer paid (including shipping) plus TikTok's platform discount, minus any refunds. TikTok's example: S$1,010 × 3.27% = S$33.027. It is refunded proportionally when an order is returned.

    3. BXP service fee (BXP sellers only)

    BXP gives your products Bonus Cashback, LIVE Specials and Voucher Xtra vouchers in exchange for a service fee on every completed order, voucher or not: 3.27% in listed electronics, baby-care and appliance sub-categories and 5.45% in all others, on (net product price − seller-funded discounts), capped at S$30 per item. During double-digit and Payday campaigns it rises by 1.09 points (4.36% / 6.54%). The calculator applies the right rate to each of the 2,035 categories (178 sit in the 3.27% group).

    TikTok replaced the 27 July 2026 table with a single flat structure:

    For a standard seller that is +1.635 points in 1,833 of 2,035 categories and +3.27 points in 202. In capped categories, any item priced above about S$305.81 (standard) or S$423.43 (BXP) hits the S$30 ceiling. Full analysis: our guide to the 1 September change.

    • Cap per item. TikTok labels the S$30 as a "fee upper cap"; we apply it per item, the same way TikTok words the BXP cap.
    • BXP-restricted products. Virtual products, infant formula and gold can't use BXP and are charged the standard rate, so leave the BXP switch off for them.
    • Not included: the 1.09% pre-order service fee, affiliate partner fees, shipping adjustments and penalties. Add any that apply under "Other costs".

    Protecting your margin

    • Reprice everyday items. Low-ticket beauty, fashion and food took the full increase to 9.81%. On a S$20 item the extra 1.635 points cost about S$0.33 per order.
    • Check BXP category by category. BXP saves 2.725 commission points but costs 3.27% or 5.45% more, so per-order fees go up. It is worth it only if the vouchers lift sales.
    • Use the cap. Bundles of capped electronics above about S$306 pay no more commission per item, which can make premium bundles more attractive.
    • Fund discounts carefully. Seller discounts reduce the commission base, but every dollar still comes out of your payout. Platform-funded discounts don't reduce commission; they are part of the transaction-fee base.

    Frequently asked questions

    What are TikTok Shop Singapore's seller fees in 2026?

    A Platform Commission Fee of 9.810% (7.085% for BXP sellers) on orders from 1 September 2026, capped at S$30 in electronics, toys and collectibles categories, and a 3.27% Transaction Fee. BXP sellers also pay a 3.27% or 5.45% BXP service fee (capped at S$30 per item). All rates include 9% GST.

    Is TikTok Shop Singapore commission the same for every category?

    Since 1 September 2026, yes: 9.810% standard and 7.085% BXP in every category of the official table. What differs is the S$30 cap, which only applies to the 'EL, Toys & Collectibles' industry.

    How is commission calculated?

    (Item price − seller discount) × rate. Shipping and TikTok's platform discounts are excluded.

    How is the 3.27% transaction fee calculated?

    On the customer's payment, including shipping, plus TikTok's platform discount, minus refunds. It is refunded proportionally on returns.

    When does the BXP service fee go up?

    During double-digit and Payday campaigns it is 1.09 points higher: 4.36% instead of 3.27%, or 6.54% instead of 5.45%.

    Do the rates include GST?

    Yes. TikTok publishes the commission, transaction and BXP service fees for Singapore inclusive of 9% GST.

    Does this calculator work for cross-border sellers?

    It uses the local-seller tables. TikTok's Global Seller notice from 3 September 2026 shows different rates in its examples, so cross-border sellers should check their own Seller Center and edit the rates.
