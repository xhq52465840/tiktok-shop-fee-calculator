Ozon FBO vs FBS vs realFBS in 2026: which fee stack you actually pay

Side-by-side Ozon fees for the three fulfilment models on the same SKU, with the 17%/23% price bands and logistics from 30 Oct 2026.

SellerFee 26 October 2026 3 min read
On this page
Price bands on FBO/FBSSame SKU, three modelsWhat we leave outOfficial sources

Key takeaways

  • FBO and FBS share the same three price-band commission rates; realFBS uses one rate.
  • Logistics + 25 ₽ last-mile apply to FBO and FBS only; FBS adds ~10 ₽ processing.
  • On a 1 500 ₽ cosmetics SKU, FBO fees 889.00 ₽, FBS 899.00 ₽, realFBS 780.00 ₽ (commission only).

Ozon’s three fulfilment models look similar in the seller cabinet but the fee stack is different. This guide walks through the same SKU under each model using the rates in force in late October 2026.

Price bands on FBO/FBS

Most product types pay 17% at ≤100 ₽, 23% at 101–300 ₽ and a category rate above 300 ₽. The calculator’s default, Косметика для ухода, is 52% above 300 ₽. realFBS ignores the low-price bands and uses that single rate.

Same SKU, three models

1 500 ₽, 1.5 L, Moscow → Moscow, product cost 450 ₽.

FBO total fees889.00 ₽
FBS total fees899.00 ₽
realFBS total fees780.00 ₽
FBO profit161.00 ₽
FBS profit151.00 ₽
realFBS profit270.00 ₽

realFBS looks cheapest on fees only because you still pay your own delivery — add that under “shipping you pay” in the calculator before you compare.

What we leave out

Non-local FBO markup, FBO Fresh, FBO PVZ (−3 pp until 1 Dec 2026), Fashion new-seller 27%, forced storage 2.50 ₽/L/day and returns. See the linked Seller Edu documents.
