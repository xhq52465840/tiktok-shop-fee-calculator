Etsy Fees for UK Sellers in 2026: 0.48% Regulatory Fee, VAT on Fees and Real Examples

Every Etsy fee for a UK shop: 6.5% transaction, 4% + £0.20 processing, the 0.48% regulatory operating fee from 22 June 2026 and VAT on Etsy fees, with worked examples.

SellerFee 26 October 2026 3 min read

Etsy's fees for UK sellers changed twice in a way most fee calculators miss: the regulatory operating fee rose to 0.48% on 22 June 2026, and Etsy adds VAT to its fees unless you've given it a VAT number. Here is the full list and what it costs, with the Etsy UK fee calculator.

Every Etsy fee for a UK shop

  • Listing fee US$0.20 (about £0.15 at the 26 Oct 2026 ECB rate).
  • Transaction fee 6.5% of item price + postage + gift wrap.
  • Payment processing 4% + £0.20 per order (UK bank account).
  • Regulatory operating fee 0.48% of the order total (0.32% until 21 June 2026).
  • VAT at 20% on those fees if you haven't added a VAT number.
  • Offsite Ads 15% or 12% on attributed orders, max US$100 per order.

Who pays VAT on Etsy fees

Etsy charges VAT on seller fees for UK sellers who haven't submitted a VAT number, and on Etsy Payments processing fees where required. Add a valid VAT number in your account and Etsy stops charging it; your monthly invoice then states that no VAT was charged. For a seller below the VAT threshold, this VAT is simply a cost — on a £25 order it adds £0.71.

How to price for it

  • Budget roughly 12–14% of the order total for Etsy fees, more on cheap items.
  • Build Offsite Ads into the price of bestsellers: a 15% fee plus VAT on it can halve the margin on that order.
  • If you become VAT-registered, add your VAT number to Etsy promptly so fees stop carrying VAT.
