TikTok Shop Vietnam Seller Fees 2026: Commission, 6% Transaction Fee and the New Ads Savings

Every TikTok Shop Vietnam seller fee in 2026: category commission for Marketplace and Mall, the 6% transaction fee and its savings tiers from 21 October, 3,000 ₫ per order, withheld tax and a worked example.

SellerFee 26 October 2026 3 min read

TikTok Shop Vietnam’s fees changed several times in 2026: a new Marketplace commission table on 3 July, a new Mall table on 20 October, and transaction fee savings for shops that advertise from 21 October. This guide lists every fee on a normal order and works through an example. All the numbers come from the TikTok Shop Vietnam fee calculator.

The fees on every order

Commission: Marketplace and Mall

Commission depends on the category level 3 of the product. Marketplace shops use the table effective 3 July 2026 and Mall shops the table effective 20 October 2026. Mall rates are usually 2 to 4 points higher: Serums & Essences pay 15.5% on Marketplace and 18.8% in Mall. Mall book categories are the exception. Since 20 October 2026 they pay 12.5%, less than the Marketplace rate. Phones and laptops are among the cheapest categories, at 2% to 3.8%.

The 6% transaction fee and the new savings

The transaction fee is 6% for orders created from 9 May 2026. It is charged on what the buyer paid plus any platform discount, so it includes the shipping the buyer paid. From 21 October 2026 TikTok Shop gives a saving based on the shop’s ads take rate (ads spend as a share of GMV) over the last 30 days. For a 199,000 ₫ serum on Marketplace:

The saving only pays for itself if the ads bring in the sales. At a 10% take rate you spend far more on ads than the 1 point you save.

Worked example

A 199,000 ₫ serum on Marketplace, product cost 60,000 ₫, free shipping for the buyer and no ads savings.

Commission fee (15.5%)−30,845 ₫
Transaction fee (6%)−11,940 ₫
Order Processing Fee−3,000 ₫
Total fees45,785 ₫
Payout153,215 ₫

Profit is 93,215 ₫, a 46.8% margin. In a Mall shop the same sale costs 52,352 ₫ in fees, leaving 86,648 ₫.

Tax for individual and household sellers

Since 1 July 2025, TikTok Shop withholds tax from individual and business household sellers: 1% VAT and 0.5% personal income tax on the item price after seller discount. On the serum that is 2,985 ₫. TikTok’s own fees include Vietnamese tax unless you are a company with a verified tax code.

Fees that apply only sometimes

  • Logistics service fee: from 12 October 2026, for parcels over 10 kg only. It starts at 3,000 ₫ and is capped at 65,000 ₫ per parcel.
  • Affiliate commission: the rate you set for creators, on affiliate orders only.
