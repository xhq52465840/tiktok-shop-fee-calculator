TikTok Shop Malaysia Commission Changes on 31 October 2026, Explained

What changes in TikTok Shop Malaysia commission on 31 Oct 2026: which categories go up or down, BXP vs non-BXP, worked examples in ringgit and how to reprice.

SellerFee 26 October 2026 3 min read
On this page
The change in numbersBiggest increasesCategories that get cheaperBXP vs non-BXP after the changeWhat it means per orderWhat to do before 31 OctoberOfficial sources

TikTok Shop Malaysia published a new commission table on 16 October 2026. It applies to orders placed from 00:00 on 31 October 2026, Malaysia time (UTC+8). Orders placed before that follow the current rates. Nothing else changes on that date: the 3.78% transaction fee, the RM0.54 Platform Support Fee and the BXP service fee stay as they are, and TikTok says there are no new flat, listing or monthly charges.

The change in numbers

Looking at non-BXP Marketplace rates across the 230 sub-categories that had a rate before:

  • 204 go up. The most common increases are +1.62 points (146 sub-categories), +0.33 points (27 sub-categories), +1.41 points (14 sub-categories).
  • 6 go down, all in essentials and vouchers.
  • 20 stay the same, including the Pre-Owned fashion lines and the 4 categories at 0%: Food & Beverages › Baking, Food & Beverages › Fresh & Frozen Food, Food & Beverages › Milk & Dairy, Food & Beverages › Staples & Cooking Essentials.
  • One new category, Beer, Wine & Spirits, gets a rate for the first time (12.21% for non-BXP Marketplace).

The highest rate in the new table is 20.52% (non-BXP Mall fragrance, makeup, men's care, nail care and hand & foot care), up from 18.90%.

BXP vs non-BXP after the change

BXP sellers still pay lower commission, but in most categories the gap narrows. Before 31 October a BXP seller paid 4.32 points less than a non-BXP seller in almost every category. From 31 October the gap is 2.70 points in most categories and 2.17 points in most clothing and bag categories. Since BXP sellers also pay the 4.86% BXP fee (7.02% on campaign days), being in BXP now costs more per order in most categories than before. The exception is Baby Care & Health, where BXP commission falls to 7.78% while the non-BXP rate rises to 13.82%.

What it means per order

RM35 hijab, non-BXP Marketplace, cost RM11

Commission goes from 14.58% to 14.91%. Payout falls from RM28.04 to RM27.92, profit from RM17.04 to RM16.92. To keep the old profit you would need to charge about RM35.20.

RM29.90 phone case, non-BXP Marketplace, cost RM8

Mobile phone accessories go from 11.34% to 12.96%. Profit per order goes from RM16.84 to RM16.35.

RM89 breast pump accessory set (Nursing & Feeding), cost RM40

One of the categories that gets cheaper: 9.72% → 6.8%. Profit rises from RM36.45 to RM39.05.

What to do before 31 October

  1. List your top 20 SKUs by revenue and check each one's sub-category in the Malaysia calculator, with "Orders from 31 Oct 2026" selected.
  2. Decide per SKU whether to absorb the increase, raise the price or bundle. For low-priced items, bundling also spreads the RM0.54 Platform Support Fee.
  3. If you are in BXP, compare your fees with BXP switched on and off. Remember the campaign-day rate of 7.02% on Double Day 8–11 November and Payday 24–25 November.
  4. After 31 October, check your first settlements against the new rates. Our settlement guide shows where to look.
