TikTok Shop Indonesia Seller Fees 2026: Commission, Dynamic Commission Fee and GMV Max Savings

Every TikTok Shop and Tokopedia seller fee in Indonesia in 2026: commission by category from 18 May, the 3–8% Dynamic Commission Fee, GMV Max and Growth Xtra savings, Rp1,250 per order, the Logistics Service Fee and a worked example.

SellerFee 26 October 2026 3 min read
On this page
The fees on every orderWorked exampleGMV Max and Growth Xtra savingsThe Logistics Service FeeFees that apply only sometimesOfficial sources

TikTok Shop and Tokopedia now share one seller platform in Indonesia, and their fees changed on 18 May 2026: a new commission table by category, a higher Dynamic Commission Fee, and lower rates for sellers who use GMV Max ads or the Growth Xtra Program. This guide lists every fee on a normal order and works through an example. All the numbers come from the TikTok Shop Indonesia fee calculator.

The fees on every order

Worked example

An Rp89,000 serum on Marketplace, product cost Rp30,000, free shipping for the buyer, no savings programme.

Commission fee (9.25%)−Rp8,233
Dynamic Commission Fee (7%)−Rp6,230
Order Processing Fee−Rp1,250
Logistics Service Fee−Rp690
Total feesRp16,403
PayoutRp72,597

Profit is Rp42,597, a 47.9% margin. Commission and the Dynamic Commission Fee together take 16.25% of the price.

GMV Max and Growth Xtra savings

Since 18 May 2026 the commission table has a column for each savings situation. GMV Max orders get a lower rate per order. A shop whose GMV Max spend (Product and LIVE GMV Max) is 3% or more of its GMV over the last 30 days gets a lower rate on every order. Growth Xtra members get a shop-wide saving too, and the biggest saving, up to 8.18 points, goes to shops that do both. For the same serum on Marketplace:

The savings are not free. GMV Max is paid advertising, and Growth Xtra has its own service fee of 2% to 4%. Check that the extra sales cover both.

The Logistics Service Fee

Since 1 May 2026 every order also pays a flat Logistics Service Fee. It depends on the shipping service, the route and the chargeable weight (the higher of actual and volumetric weight, length × width × height in cm ÷ 6,000). Standard shipping:

Economy, cargo and instant or same-day shipping have their own rates. The fee is not refunded when a delivered order is returned.

Fees that apply only sometimes

  • Pre-order service fee: 3% of the item price after seller discount, on pre-order products.
  • Growth Xtra Program fee: 2% to 4% by category for members, plus 2% (up to Rp20,000 per item) for GXP Super.
  • Affiliate commission: the rate you set for creators, on affiliate orders only.
