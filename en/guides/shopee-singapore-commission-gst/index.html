Shopee Singapore Commission and Transaction Fees (GST-inclusive), Explained

Shopee SG Mall vs non-Mall commission from 28 August 2026, the 3.27% GST-inclusive transaction fee, PXP preferential rates and per-item caps.

SellerFee 26 October 2026 3 min read
On this page
28 August 2026 reductionPXP in one lineOfficial sources

Shopee Singapore publishes GST-inclusive commission and a 3.27% GST-inclusive transaction fee. Preferential PXP commission sits beside a separate PXP service fee. Use the Shopee Singapore fee calculator for payout and margin.

28 August 2026 reduction

Selected Food & Beverages, toilet paper, books and musical instruments dropped by 1 percentage point. Electronics keep per-item caps (commonly S$60 non-Mall / S$120 Mall).

PXP in one line

Lower commission column + category service fee (enter yours; FAQ example 5.45%). Run both sides in the calculator before joining.
