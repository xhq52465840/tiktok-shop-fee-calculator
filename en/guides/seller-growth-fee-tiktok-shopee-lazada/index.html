TikTok Shop vs Shopee vs Lazada Seller Growth Fees 2026: Malaysia, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand

Which platforms charge a seller growth fee in Southeast Asia, at what rate: TikTok Shop’s Seller Growth Fee and Commerce Growth Fee, Shopee’s Seller Growth Support Fee, and what Lazada charges, by country.

SellerFee 26 October 2026 3 min read
On this page

TikTok Shop and Shopee now both charge a per-order “growth” fee in some Southeast Asian markets, on top of commission and the transaction fee. The names are similar but the rates, categories and caps are not. This page compares them market by market, using each platform’s own seller centre pages, and links to the calculator that includes each fee.

Growth fees by market

The secondary sources we checked list no Lazada growth fee in Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore or Thailand, but Lazada’s full fee schedules for these markets sit behind Seller Center login or are published as images, so we could not verify them from an official page. We will add Lazada once we can.

Philippines: two fees with the same idea, different categories

Both platforms charge 1% or 1.5%, but they sort categories differently. On TikTok Shop fashion pays 1.5%; on Shopee, women’s clothes pay 1.00%. On a ₱499 top the TikTok Shop growth fee is ₱7.49 and Shopee’s is ₱5.00 (Shopee rounds fees to the peso). Home items pay 1.5% on both: ₱7.00 on Shopee for a ₱499 home item.

AMZ123 reported on 27 October 2026 that TikTok Shop Philippines has announced a change from 1 November 2026: the growth fee is charged on every successful order for all sellers, and Lifestyle products (except Large Appliances) go from 1% to 1.5%. See our TikTok Shop Philippines Seller Growth Fee guide for the details.

Thailand: the biggest growth fee by far

TikTok Shop Thailand’s Commerce Growth Fee is several times larger than the Philippine fees. A ฿299 skincare item pays ฿24.01 (8.03%). The ฿199 cap matters on expensive items: a ฿5,000 electronics item would pay ฿348 at 6.96%, but the cap limits it to ฿199.00. New sellers do not pay it for their first 30 days (capped at ฿1,000 for sellers who joined after 16 July 2026), and sellers who ship only with their own fleet are not charged.

What to do about it

  • Check your category on each platform. The same product can pay 1% on one platform and 1.5% on the other.
  • Price for the fee, not around it. None of these fees has a general exemption beyond the new-seller windows, so build them into your price.
  • Watch the cap in Thailand. Above about ฿2,480 per item, TikTok Shop Thailand’s fee stops growing.
