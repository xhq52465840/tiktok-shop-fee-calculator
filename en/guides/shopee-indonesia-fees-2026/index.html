Shopee Indonesia Seller Fees 2026: Admin Fee, Gratis Ongkir XTRA from 1 December and Mall Fees

Every Shopee Indonesia seller fee in 2026: admin fee for Non-Star, Star/Star+ and Mall by product type, the 1.8% Mall payment fee, Rp1,250 per order, the new Gratis Ongkir XTRA rates from 1 December 2026 and worked examples.

SellerFee 26 October 2026 3 min read
On this page

Shopee Indonesia’s fees depend on your seller type and the product type. In 2026 the new-seller free period ended for sellers starting from 1 August, and the optional Gratis Ongkir XTRA programme gets new, higher rates from 1 December 2026 (1 January 2027 for Mall). This guide lists every fee and works through examples. All the numbers come from the Shopee Indonesia fee calculator.

The fees on every order

FeeNon-Star and Star/Star+Shopee Mall
Admin fee2.5% to 10% by product type2.5% to 11.7% by product type
Payment feeNone1.8%, up to Rp50,000 per item
Order Processing FeeRp1,250 per completed orderRp1,250 per completed order

Percentage fees are charged on the original price minus seller discounts and seller vouchers. Shipping is never included.

Worked example

An Rp89,000 face care product from a Non-Star or Star seller, product cost Rp30,000, free shipping for the buyer, no optional programmes.

Admin fee (8.25%)−Rp7,343
Order Processing Fee−Rp1,250
Total feesRp8,593
PayoutRp80,407

Profit is Rp50,407, a 56.6% margin.

Gratis Ongkir XTRA from 1 December 2026

Gratis Ongkir XTRA gives your products free-shipping vouchers in exchange for a fee by product type. From 1 December 2026 (non-Mall) and 1 January 2027 (Mall), most regular-size rates rise by 3 to 3.5 points and most special-size rates by 3.5 to 4. The cap per item goes from Rp40,000 to Rp60,000 for regular-size products and from Rp60,000 to Rp80,000 for special-size products. For the same face care product (5.5% now, 9% from December):

SituationFeesProfit
Not in Gratis Ongkir XTRARp8,593Rp50,407
Gratis Ongkir XTRA, current rateRp13,488Rp45,512
Gratis Ongkir XTRA from 1 Dec 2026Rp16,603Rp42,397

The higher cap matters for expensive products. On a Rp1,500,000 item the fee is capped at Rp40,000 now. From December it is capped at Rp60,000: total fees go from Rp165,000 to Rp185,000.

The new-seller free period

Non-Star sellers who uploaded their first product before 1 August 2026 pay no admin fee and no Order Processing Fee until 50 completed orders or 6 months, whichever comes first. Sellers who start on or after 1 August 2026 pay the normal fees from the first order. Shopee runs separate support programmes for new small businesses instead.

Other optional fees

  • Promo XTRA: 4.5% (max Rp60,000 per item), or 6.5% for Promo XTRA+ (max Rp80,000).
  • Live/Video XTRA: 3% on orders through Shopee Live or Shopee Video, or 2% with Promo XTRA, max Rp20,000 per item.
  • Pre-order service fee: 3% on pre-order products.
  • SPayLater XTRA 0%: 2.5% (3-month) or 4% (6-month) on orders paid with SPayLater instalments.
