Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

This policy explains what information SellerFee ("we") collects when you use this website and how it is used.

Information you enter in calculators

The calculators run in your browser. Prices, costs and other numbers you enter are not sent to or stored on our servers.

Information collected automatically

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and operational purposes. See GitHub's privacy statement for details. We do not currently run our own analytics.

Cookies

SellerFee itself does not set cookies today. If we add analytics or advertising, the third-party services described below may use cookies or similar technologies, and we will update this policy before doing so.

Advertising

We may show ads served by third-party vendors, including Google. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info. Where required by law, we will ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to official marketplace help pages and other sites. Their privacy practices are their own.

Children

The site is intended for adults running a business and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised through our contact page.
