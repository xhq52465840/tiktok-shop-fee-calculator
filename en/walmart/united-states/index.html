    How Walmart Marketplace fees work

    Walmart Marketplace (US) charges no monthly subscription and no setup fee. On each sale you pay a referral fee: a percentage of the total sales price, which includes the item price plus any shipping, handling and gift-wrap charges paid by the customer. Walmart deducts it from your payout. If you use Walmart Fulfillment Services (WFS), fulfillment and storage fees are added on top.

    1. Referral fee

    Most categories pay a flat rate, usually 15%. Some categories use one of two other rules, and the calculator applies both automatically:

    • Whole-price bands. One rate applies to the whole item, depending on its total sales price. For example, Beauty, Health & Personal Care pays 8% if the item sells for $10 or less and 15% above $10.
    • Portion tiers. One rate applies to the part of the price up to a threshold and a second rate to the part above it. For example, Electronics Accessories pays 15% on the first $100 and 8% on the rest.

    Some secondary sources report that Walmart lowered rates in several categories from June 2026, but Walmart’s published schedule above has not changed. Each rate in the calculator can be edited, so check the commission rate column in your Seller Center payment statements and enter your own rate if it differs.

    New-Seller Savings 2026

    First-time sellers who go live after 1 February 2026 and opt in through the Seller Center form get a discount on referral fees. The discount depends on GMV (gross merchandise value) since launch:

    GMV since launchReferral fee discount
    $0.01–$50,00020%
    $50,001–$500,00030%
    Over $500,00040%

    The program runs from 2 February 2026 to 31 January 2027. Discounts stop on 31 January 2027 or when you reach the $72,000 maximum, whichever comes first. Sellers who joined between 1 February and 27 April 2026 do not need to enroll by clicking. Eligible sellers using WFS also get 10% of their fulfillment fees back, up to $2,000. The offer includes other incentives as well (a sponsored-search ad rebate and Walmart Connect credits); they don’t change per-order fees, so the calculator leaves them out. Choose your tier in the calculator to see the discount as a separate credit line.

    Walmart Fulfillment Services fees

    WFS charges a fulfillment fee per unit, based on shipping weight. Under 1 lb, Walmart adds 0.25 lb for packaging to the unit weight and rounds up. From 1 lb to 150 lb, the shipping weight is the greater of the unit weight and the dimensional weight (length × width × height in inches ÷ 139), rounded up to the next whole pound. The standard base fees, in effect since April 2025, are:

    Shipping weightFee per unit
    Up to 1 lb$3.45
    2 lb$4.95
    3 lb$5.45
    4–20 lb$5.75 + $0.40 per lb above 4 lb
    21–30 lb$15.55 + $0.40 per lb above 21 lb
    31–50 lb$14.55 + $0.40 per lb above 31 lb
    51–150 lb$17.55 + $0.40 per lb above 51 lb

    Walmart’s published table shows a lower starting fee for 31–50 lb ($14.55) than for 21–30 lb ($15.55). The calculator uses the table exactly as published.

    Surcharges per unit: apparel $0.50, hazardous materials $0.50, items priced under $10 $1.00, oversize $3.00 (longest side over 48 in, median side over 30 in, or longest side + girth over 105 in). Larger oversize items ($20, with weight counted as at least 90 lb) and Big & Bulky items ($155 plus $0.80 for each pound above 90 lb) follow different rules and are not covered here. Storage costs $0.75 per cubic foot per month, rising after 30 days in October–December. From 30 June 2026, long-term storage is $2.25 per cubic foot for 366–450 days and $7.50 above 450 days. Enter storage under other costs.

    A 1.5 lb unit rounds up to 2 lb, so the base fee is $4.95.

    WFS fulfillment fee−$3.45
    WFS apparel surcharge−$0.50

    Frequently asked questions

    Does Walmart Marketplace charge a monthly fee?

    No. Walmart Marketplace has no monthly subscription and no setup fee. You pay a referral fee on each sale, plus WFS fees if you use Walmart fulfillment.

    Is the referral fee charged on shipping?

    Yes. Walmart calculates the referral fee on the total sales price, which includes the item price and any shipping, handling and gift-wrap charges paid by the customer.

    How does the 2026 New-Seller Savings discount work?

    First-time sellers who go live after 1 February 2026 and opt in get 20%, 30% or 40% off referral fees depending on GMV since launch, until 31 January 2027 or a $72,000 maximum. Eligible WFS users also get 10% of fulfillment fees back, up to $2,000.

    How is the WFS shipping weight calculated?

    Under 1 lb, Walmart adds 0.25 lb to the unit weight and rounds up. From 1 lb, it takes the greater of the unit weight and the dimensional weight (L × W × H ÷ 139) and rounds up to the next pound.

    Why does my statement show a different referral rate?

    Some categories may be on a contract or updated rate. Every rate in the calculator can be edited, so enter the commission rate shown in your Seller Center statements.
