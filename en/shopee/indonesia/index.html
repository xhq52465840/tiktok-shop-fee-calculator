    How Shopee Indonesia fees work

    Shopee Indonesia charges by seller type: Non-Star, Star/Star+ and Shopee Mall. Non-Star and Star sellers pay the same rates. All fees include VAT and are charged on the item price after seller discounts and seller vouchers, never on shipping.

    1. Admin fee

    The admin fee (Biaya Administrasi) depends on the product type: 2.5% to 10% for Non-Star and Star sellers and 2.5% to 11.7% for Shopee Mall. There is no cap per item. The 10% top rate is already 20% below the 12.5% base rate.

    Non-Star sellers who uploaded their first product before 1 August 2026 pay no admin fee and no Order Processing Fee until they complete 50 orders or reach 6 months. Sellers who started on or after 1 August 2026 pay from the first order.

    2. Shopee Mall payment fee

    Mall sellers also pay 1.8%, capped at Rp50,000 per item, in every category.

    3. Order Processing Fee

    Rp1,250 per completed order. On a multi-product order it is split across the products by value.

    4. Optional programmes

    Gratis Ongkir XTRA (free shipping) charges a rate by product type: 1% to 8% for regular-size products, capped at Rp40,000 per item, and up to 9.5% for special-size products, capped at Rp60,000. New rates apply from 1 December 2026 for non-Mall sellers and from 1 January 2027 for Mall: up to 11% with a Rp60,000 cap for regular size and up to 13% with an Rp80,000 cap for special size. Choose the current or the new rates in the calculator.

    Promo XTRA costs 4.5% (max Rp60,000 per item) and Promo XTRA+ 6.5% (max Rp80,000). Orders through Shopee Live or Shopee Video pay 3%, or 2% if you are in Promo XTRA, capped at Rp20,000 per item. Pre-order products pay another 3%.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is the Shopee Indonesia admin fee?

    2.5% to 10% of the item price for Non-Star and Star/Star+ sellers and 2.5% to 11.7% for Shopee Mall, by product type, with no cap per item.

    Do Star sellers pay less than Non-Star sellers?

    No. Non-Star and Star/Star+ sellers pay the same admin fee rates. Only new Non-Star sellers who started before 1 August 2026 still get the free period.

    Is Gratis Ongkir XTRA compulsory?

    No. It is an optional programme. You pay its fee only if you join, and you can leave at any time.

    What changes on 1 December 2026?

    New Gratis Ongkir XTRA rates for non-Mall sellers, with Mall following on 1 January 2027. Most regular-size rates go up by 3 to 3.5 points (special size by 3.5 to 4) and the caps rise from Rp40,000 to Rp60,000 (regular size) and from Rp60,000 to Rp80,000 (special size).

    Is there a fee per order?

    Yes. The Order Processing Fee is Rp1,250 per completed order.
