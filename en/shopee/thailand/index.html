    How Shopee Thailand fees work

    Shopee Thailand’s fee policy covers three charges on a normal completed order. All rates below include 7% VAT.

    1. Sale transaction fee (commission)

    New category tables apply from 4 August 2026, with separate tables for Non-Mall and Mall sellers. Each table lists more than 1,400 categories and four columns: standard, PRIME seller, small seller and small seller + PRIME. Shopee defines a small seller as one with sales of ฿10,000 or less in the previous 30 days. The rate is applied to the item price after seller discounts; shipping is not included. The calculator loads both full tables, so search for your category and choose your seller type.

    2. Payment transaction fee

    3% + VAT = 3.21% of the order amount, including shipping and any Shopee-funded discounts or coins. Instalment and some SPayLater payments cost more. To model those, edit the rate.

    3. Platform Infrastructure Fee

    ฿1 + VAT = ฿1.07 per completed order.

    4. Programme service fees

    Programmes such as free-shipping or coins-cashback promotions have their own service fees. They are optional and vary by programme, so enter your total percentage in the field provided.

    Frequently asked questions

    When did the new Shopee Thailand commission start?

    Shopee Thailand’s new Non-Mall and Mall sale transaction fee tables apply from 4 August 2026.

    Do Shopee Thailand fees include VAT?

    Yes. The category rates include 7% VAT. The payment transaction fee is 3% + VAT (3.21%), and the Platform Infrastructure Fee is ฿1 + VAT (฿1.07) per order.

    What is a small seller?

    Shopee’s fee tables give a lower rate to small sellers, defined as sellers with sales of ฿10,000 or less in the previous 30 days.

    Is commission charged on shipping?

    No. Commission is charged on the item price after seller discounts. The 3.21% payment transaction fee does include shipping.
