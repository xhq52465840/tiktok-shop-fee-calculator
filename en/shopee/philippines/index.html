    How Shopee Philippines fees work

    Shopee PH publishes every seller fee as VAT-inclusive and rounds each fee to the nearest peso. The calculator does the same. These fees apply to a completed order:

    1. Commission fee

    Commission is a category rate applied to the item price after seller discounts. The Marketplace table has applied since 1 June 2025, with rates from 8.50% (mobile phones, cameras) to 10.50% (clothes, home & living, sports). Mall uses a separate, more detailed table that has applied since 1 January 2025. New sellers pay no commission for their first 90 days.

    2. Transaction fee

    2.24% of the total amount the buyer pays, including shipping. If the buyer uses SPayLater instalments, an extra 3–6% applies. To model that, edit the transaction fee rate.

    3. Platform Shipping Fee

    5.6% of the item price after discounts for Marketplace sellers, or 4.48% for Mall, up to ₱100 per item. It applies to orders shipped with Shopee-supported logistics.

    4. Seller Growth Support Fee

    1.00% or 1.50% of the item price after discounts, depending on the category (list updated 22 October 2026). New sellers are exempt for their first 90 days. See how it compares with TikTok Shop’s growth fees in our growth fee comparison.

    5. Order Processing Fee

    ₱5 per completed order. You don’t pay it on your first 50 orders each month, or during a new seller’s first 90 days.

    6. Optional programmes

    If you join Mega Discount Voucher, it adds a 4% service fee, up to ₱200 per item, with 1% more on spike campaign days. Live Xtra (3.36%, up to ₱100) and pre-order (2%) have their own fees. Enter those under other costs or edit a rate.

    Example 1: Shopee’s own sample computation (Mall, mobile phone)

    Price ₱400 with ₱30 in seller discounts, so the item price after discounts is ₱370. The buyer pays ₱420 including ₱50 shipping, and the seller has joined Mega Discount Voucher. Commission is 6.12%.

    Commission fee (6.12%)−₱23.00
    Transaction fee (2.24%)−₱9.00
    Platform Shipping Fee (4.48%)−₱17.00
    Seller Growth Support Fee (1%)−₱4.00
    Order Processing Fee−₱5.00
    Mega Discount Voucher service fee (4%)−₱15.00
    Total fees₱73.00
    Payout₱297.00

    The ₱50 shipping goes to the courier, so we enter it as shipping cost too. Net income: ₱297.00, matching Shopee’s published result of ₱297.

    Example 2: ₱499 dress, Marketplace

    Women Clothes at 10.50%, product cost ₱180, no shipping paid by the buyer.

    Commission fee (10.5%)−₱52.00
    Transaction fee (2.24%)−₱11.00
    Platform Shipping Fee (5.6%)−₱28.00
    Seller Growth Support Fee (1%)−₱5.00

    Profit ₱218.00 (43.7% margin). In the first 90 days the new-seller exemptions remove commission, Seller Growth Support Fee and Order Processing Fee, so fees drop to ₱39.00.

    At 8.50%, commission is ₱1,105.00. The Platform Shipping Fee is capped at ₱100.00. Total fees: ₱1,631.00.

    Protecting your margin

    • Plan for the end of the 90-day exemption. When it ends, commission, the growth support fee and the order fee all start at once.
    • Check the ₱100 shipping-fee cap on higher-priced items. Above about ₱1,786 (Marketplace) the cap applies.
    • Only add Mega Discount Voucher if the extra orders outweigh the 4% fee.

    Frequently asked questions

    Are Shopee Philippines fees VAT-inclusive?

    Yes. Shopee PH publishes its commission, transaction, platform shipping and other fees as VAT-inclusive, and rounds each fee to the nearest peso.

    What is the Shopee PH transaction fee?

    2.24% of the total amount the buyer pays, including shipping. SPayLater instalment orders carry an extra 3–6%.

    What is the Platform Shipping Fee?

    5.6% of the item price after discounts for Marketplace sellers, or 4.48% for Mall, up to ₱100 per item, on orders shipped with Shopee-supported logistics.

    Do new sellers pay commission?

    No. New sellers pay no commission, Seller Growth Support Fee or Order Processing Fee for their first 90 days. The transaction fee and Platform Shipping Fee still apply.

    When is the ₱5 Order Processing Fee waived?

    On your first 50 orders each month, and during a new seller’s first 90 days.
