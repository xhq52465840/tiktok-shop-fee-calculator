    How Shopee Singapore fees work

    Local Shopee SG sellers pay a GST-inclusive category commission and a GST-inclusive transaction fee on every successful order. Sellers in the Promo Xtra Programme (PXP) get preferential commission rates and pay a separate PXP service fee.

    1. Commission fee

    GST-inclusive percentage of the product price after seller-sponsored promotions. Mall and non-Mall (Marketplace) have different rates; PXP sellers use the preferential column. Selected electronics and fashion categories have per-item caps (e.g. S$60 non-Mall / S$120 Mall for many electronics). From 28 August 2026, selected food, home-care, books and musical-instrument categories received a 1 percentage-point reduction.

    2. Transaction fee

    3.27% (GST-inclusive) of the final payment amount, including buyer-paid shipping. SPayLater can range from 3.27% to 6.54% depending on the buyer's repayment period — the calculator defaults to 3.27%; edit the rate for longer tenures.

    3. PXP service fee

    Charged only if you join Promo Xtra. The percentage varies by category (Shopee's FAQ example uses 5.45% of gross settlement). Enter your category rate under fee rates — we do not invent a category table we have not verified.

    4. New-seller waiver

    Eligible new Mall and non-Mall sellers get 0% commission for the first 120 days of seller activity. PXP service fees have a separate 30-day waiver for new-to-Shopee sellers who join PXP.

    Example 3: S$500 phone, non-Mall — commission cap

    At 11.99% the uncapped commission would be over S$60; the published non-Mall cap is S$60 per item. Calculator commission: S$59.95.

    Protecting your margin

    • Watch the per-item caps on electronics. High-ticket phones and appliances often hit the S$60 / S$120 caps, which helps margin versus an uncapped percentage.
    • Model SPayLater separately if a large share of buyers use longer tenures (up to 6.54%).
    • Enter your real PXP service fee from Seller Center before deciding whether the preferential commission is worth it.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the Shopee Singapore transaction fee?

    3.27% GST-inclusive of the final payment amount (including buyer-paid shipping). SPayLater can be higher, up to 6.54%.

    Do commission rates include GST?

    Yes. Shopee SG publishes commission and transaction fees as GST-inclusive.

    What changed on 28 August 2026?

    Shopee reduced commission by 1 percentage point for selected Food & Beverages, Home & Living (toilet paper), books and musical instruments.

    How does the 120-day new-seller waiver work?

    Eligible new Mall and non-Mall sellers pay 0% commission for the first 120 days of seller activity. Service and transaction fees still apply.

    Where do PXP service fee rates come from?

    They vary by category on a separate Shopee page. Enter the rate shown in your Seller Center; the FAQ example of 5.45% is only a placeholder.
