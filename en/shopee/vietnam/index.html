    How Shopee Vietnam fees work

    Every delivered Shopee Vietnam order pays three fees. All rates include VAT.

    1. Fixed fee (commission)

    The fixed fee (Phí Cố Định) is Shopee’s commission, a percentage by level-3 category, charged on each item’s value: 1.5% to 17% for non-Mall shops from the table effective 23 May 2026, and 1.5% to 20.8% for Shopee Mall from the table effective 4 October 2026. New non-Mall shops pay no fixed fee for the first 30 days after their first product goes live.

    2. Transaction processing fee

    6% for every payment method (Phí Xử Lý Giao Dịch, renamed from Phí Thanh Toán in April 2026). It is charged on the item price plus the shipping the buyer paid, minus seller promotions.

    3. Infrastructure fee

    3,000 ₫ per delivered order (Phí Hạ Tầng).

    Optional fees and tax

    Voucher Xtra costs 5.5% of each item, capped at 50,000 ₫ per item, and PiShip 2,700 ₫ per order. For individual and business household sellers Shopee also withholds 1% VAT and 0.5% personal income tax on the item price after seller discount.

    A few vehicle-parts categories are named differently in the non-Mall and Mall tables, so they show a rate for one shop type only.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much is the Shopee Vietnam commission?

    The fixed fee is 1.5% to 17% for non-Mall shops and 1.5% to 20.8% for Shopee Mall, by category. On top of that there is a 6% transaction processing fee and 3,000 ₫ per order.

    Is the Shopee Vietnam payment fee 6%?

    Yes. Since 1 May 2026 (non-Mall) and 8 May 2026 (Mall) the transaction processing fee is 6% for every payment method, including COD. It includes the shipping the buyer paid.

    Does Shopee withhold tax?

    For individual and business household sellers, yes: 1% VAT and 0.5% personal income tax on goods, since 1 July 2025. Registered companies declare their own tax.

    Do new shops pay the fixed fee?

    New non-Mall shops pay no fixed fee for 30 days after their first product goes live. The other fees still apply.
