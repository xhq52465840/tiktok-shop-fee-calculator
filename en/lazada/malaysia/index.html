    How Lazada Malaysia fees work

    Lazada Malaysia charges a category commission on delivered orders. From 14 August 2026 the Marketplace and LazMall tables were revised. Published rates are subject to 8% SST; this calculator stores SST-inclusive floors (list × 1.08) as the default and keeps the cluster ceiling in the rate table so you can edit up.

    Lazada's public help centre serves the commission page as a JavaScript app that returned no readable table to our scraper. The cluster ranges below come from a dated BigSeller reprint of Lazada's official 14 August 2026 Marketplace and LazMall tables, cross-checked against the Seller Center path Settings → Commission. Every rate field is editable — paste the exact % from your Seller Center when you have it.

    1. Commission fee

    Percentage of item price after seller-funded discounts, by product cluster (General Merchandise, FMCG, Fashion, Electronics, Digital Goods). LazMall is higher than Marketplace in most clusters. New sellers typically get a 120-day commission-free window — use the toggle.

    2. Payment and other fees

    Lazada may charge payment or campaign-related fees on top of commission. We have not verified a single published payment-fee percentage for all MY sellers, so the field starts empty and is editable.

    What we could not verify

    • Per-sub-category rates inside each cluster — only cluster ranges are published in the secondary reprint we could read.
    • A single official payment-fee % for all MY sellers — enter yours from the fee statement.
    • Live scrape of sellercenter.lazada.com.my — the commission FAQ is a client-rendered page that returned an empty body to automation.

    When Lazada publishes a stable public HTML or PDF table, we will replace cluster floors with per-category rows.

    Frequently asked questions

    Where do these Lazada MY rates come from?

    Cluster ranges effective 14 August 2026, subject to 8% SST, as reprinted by BigSeller from Lazada's Marketplace and LazMall commission tables. Confirm the exact % under Seller Center → Settings → Commission.

    Why only cluster ranges, not every sub-category?

    Lazada's public help page did not expose a readable per-category table to our scraper. We refuse to invent rows, so the calculator uses verified cluster floors with editable rates.

    Are rates inclusive of SST?

    Lazada labels the tables 'subject to 8% SST'. We multiply by 1.08 so the default matches an all-in deduction; edit if your statement shows the ex-SST figure.

    Is there a new-seller waiver?

    Lazada commonly offers about 120 commission-free days for new sellers. Use the toggle; confirm the exact window in your onboarding email or Seller Center.
