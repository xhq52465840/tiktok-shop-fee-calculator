    Wildberries charges a subject-level commission (КВВ) that differs for FBW, FBS and DBS, plus volume-based logistics on FBW/FBS. Acquiring is not published as a fixed public percentage.

    FBW, FBS and DBS

    • FBW — stock in a WB warehouse. Commission + logistics (base tariff × warehouse coefficient).
    • FBS — you ship to WB. Higher КВВ than FBW on most subjects; same logistics formula (coefficient may differ by warehouse — check your cabinet). Optional fast-shipping discounts on commission.
    • DBS — you deliver to the buyer. Commission only.

    Logistics formula

    For volumes up to 1 L WB publishes fixed steps (0.2 L → 23 ₽ … 1 L → 32 ₽). Above 1 L: 46 ₽ for the first litre + 14 ₽ per extra litre, then × the warehouse coefficient (170% for WB warehouses since 15 Aug 2026). Reverse delivery uses the base tariff without the coefficient.

    0.55 L × 170%29 ₽ × 1.70 = 49.30 ₽ (official worked example)
    1.8 L × 170%(46 + 14 × 0.8) × 1.70 = 97.24 ₽

    Frequently asked questions

    What is КВВ?

    Комиссия вознаграждения Wildberries — the subject-level commission percentage. The official PDF lists separate FBW / FBS / DBS values per subject, in force from 7 July 2026.

    How is logistics calculated?

    Stepped tariff to 1 L, then 46 ₽ + 14 ₽ × extra litres, multiplied by the warehouse coefficient (170% for WB warehouses since 15 Aug 2026). Official examples: 0.55 L → 49.30 ₽, 1.8 L → 97.24 ₽ at 170%.

    Do FBS speed discounts apply?

    Kommersant (7 Oct 2026) and seller tools report −5 pp for dispatch under 13 hours and −3.5 pp under 42 hours. They are not in the КВВ PDF itself, so treat them as editable and confirm in your account.

    Is acquiring included?

    No public fixed acquiring % is published. Leave the field blank or enter your contract rate.
