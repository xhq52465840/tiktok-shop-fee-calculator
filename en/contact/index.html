Contact SellerFee

We'd like to hear from you, especially if a platform has changed its fees and we haven't caught it yet.

Report a rate change or an error

Open an issue on our public project page: github.com/xhq52465840/tiktok-shop-fee-calculator/issues. Please include the platform, the market, the fee that looks wrong and, if you can, a link to the official page that shows the current rate.

Request a calculator

Use the same page to request a platform or market. We add markets once their rates can be verified from official sources.

What we can't help with

We can't see or change anything in your marketplace account. For questions about a specific order or deduction, contact the platform's seller support, for example through Seller Center → Help Center on TikTok Shop.
