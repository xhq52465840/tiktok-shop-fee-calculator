About SellerFee

SellerFee is an independent website that publishes free fee and profit calculators for people who sell on online marketplaces, especially sellers working across borders. We started with TikTok Shop in Malaysia and the Philippines, where fees changed several times in 2026, and are adding more platforms and markets.

How we source rates

Every rate in our calculators comes from the platform's own seller help pages, such as TikTok Shop Seller University or the Etsy Help Center. Each calculator links its sources and shows the date we last checked them. When a platform publishes only a rate but not how it is calculated, we say so on the page and make the rate editable. We don't publish a calculator for a market until its rates can be verified from an official source.

How we keep them current

We re-check the source pages regularly and whenever a platform announces a change. When fees change, we keep the old rates alongside the new ones so you can compare, and we note the effective date.

How the calculators work

The calculators run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us. Results are estimates based on the published rates; your own account may have waivers, program fees or promotions that change the numbers, so your Seller Center is always the final word.

Independence

SellerFee is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by TikTok, ByteDance, Etsy, Shopee, Lazada, Amazon, eBay or any other marketplace. Product and company names are trademarks of their owners.
