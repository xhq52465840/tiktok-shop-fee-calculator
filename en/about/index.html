About SellerFee

SellerFee is an independent website that publishes free fee and profit calculators for people who sell on online marketplaces, especially sellers working across borders. We cover TikTok Shop (Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, the Philippines, the US and the UK), Shopee (Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, Thailand), Lazada Malaysia, Walmart Marketplace (US), Etsy (global and UK), and the Russian marketplaces Ozon, Wildberries and Yandex Market.

How we source rates

Every rate comes from the platform’s own seller help — TikTok Shop Seller University, Shopee Seller Education Hub, Lazada Seller Center, Walmart Marketplace Learn, Etsy Help, Ozon Seller Edu, Wildberries instructions and КВВ PDF, Yandex Market help. Each calculator links its sources and shows the date we last checked them. When a platform publishes a rate but not the formula, we say so and make the rate editable. We don’t publish a calculator until rates can be verified from an official source.

How we keep them current

We re-check source pages regularly and whenever a platform announces a change. When fees change, we keep old rates alongside new ones where the platform still publishes both, and we note the effective date on Rate changes.

How the calculators work

The calculators run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us. Results are estimates based on published rates; your account may have waivers, program fees or promotions that change the numbers, so your seller cabinet is always the final word.

Independence

SellerFee is not affiliated with TikTok, Shopee, Lazada, Walmart, Etsy, Ozon, Wildberries, Yandex or any other marketplace. We don’t take listing fees or referral kickbacks for calculator results.

Found a wrong rate? Open a GitHub issue with the page and the official source.
