    Yandex Market charges category placement (usually the same across models; some rows differ for FBY/FBS/Express/DBS), plus delivery 5% (cap 1 000 ₽/item), middle-mile by volume, FBS processing 25 ₽, payment transfer 1.6–3.3% by payout schedule, and 0.12 ₽ acceptance per distinct SKU. Up to 99% of placement can be paid with Market points; placement is refunded on returns.

    FBY, FBS and DBS

    • FBY — stock in a Market warehouse. Placement + delivery + middle mile + payment fees. Storage is free from 11 Oct to 31 Dec 2026.
    • FBS — you ship to Market. Same stack plus 25 ₽ processing; optional speed discounts −4 / −7 pp on placement. Courier visit fee by daily order volume is not modelled.
    • DBS — you deliver. Placement + payment fees only.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is the 42% low-price tariff?

    Until the end of 2026, FBY/FBS items priced ≤300 ₽ with volume ≤5 L pay 42% placement and no delivery or middle-mile fee. Confirmed by the official worked example (250 ₽ → 105 ₽ placement).

    How is middle mile calculated?

    Ceil litres: 92 ₽ for the first litre, +8 ₽ per litre to 200 L, then +5 ₽, capped at 5 500 ₽. Official example: 37 L → 380 ₽.

    What are the payment transfer rates?

    From the acquiring help page: 1.6% (every 4 weeks), 2.3% (every 2 weeks), 2.8% (weekly), 3.3% (daily), plus 0.12 ₽ acceptance per distinct SKU.

    Can I pay placement with Market points?

    Yes — up to 99% of placement. Placement is refunded if the order is returned.
