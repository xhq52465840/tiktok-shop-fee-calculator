Embed a fee calculator on your site

Put any of our 21 calculators on your blog, course or agency site. Copy one snippet, paste it into your HTML, and your readers get a live calculator that we keep up to date when platforms change their fees.

Why embed it

  • Free, no account and no API key.
  • Rates are updated on our side, so the widget on your page is always current.
  • Responsive: one column on phones, two columns on wide screens. The script version sets its own height.
  • Runs in the visitor’s browser. Nothing they type is sent to us or to you, and the widget sets no cookies.

How to add it

  1. Pick a calculator and a language above, then copy the code.
  2. Paste it into your page where the calculator should appear. In WordPress use a Custom HTML block; in Webflow, Wix or Squarespace use an Embed / Code block.
  3. Keep the “Powered by sellerfee.app” line under the widget. It is the only thing we ask in return, and it tells your readers where the rates come from.

  • Don’t frame the widget in a way that hides its source or suggests it is your own tool.

Questions

Does the widget slow my page down?

The script is under 2 KB and loads the calculator lazily, only when it scrolls into view. The calculator itself is cached by the browser.

Can I change the size?

The widget takes the full width of its container. Put it in a narrower column if you want it smaller. With the plain iframe, set the height attribute yourself (about 1,900 px fits every calculator).

Will the numbers stay current?

Yes. The widget loads the same rate cards as our calculator pages, so when a platform changes its fees, your embed updates too.
