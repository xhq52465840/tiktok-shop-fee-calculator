FAQ

What are TikTok Shop Philippines seller fees in 2026?

Each delivered order is charged a commission fee by category (Marketplace 5%–7.3%, Mall 5%–9.1%), a 2.24% transaction fee, a Shipping Service Fee of 5.5% (Marketplace) or 5% (Mall) capped at ₱100 per item, a fixed ₱5 Order Processing Fee per order, and a Seller Growth Fee of 1% or 1.5% depending on category. All rates are VAT inclusive.

What is the TikTok Shop Seller Growth Fee?

A per-order fee TikTok Shop Philippines charges all sellers to fund platform-wide growth incentives, including making the Bonus Cashback Program free for eligible sellers. It is 1% for Electronics, Grocery and Large Appliances, and 1.5% for Beauty & Personal Care, Fashion, Mom & Baby, Health and Lifestyle (except Large Appliances), VAT inclusive. Seller reports date it from 27 April 2026.

Is the TikTok Shop order processing fee ₱3 or ₱5?

₱5. TikTok charged a discounted ₱3 from 1 December 2025, then ended the discount from 00:00 on 1 September 2026 (Philippine time). The fee is now a fixed ₱5 (tax inclusive) per successfully delivered order, regardless of order value or number of items.

Who doesn't pay the ₱5 order processing fee?

TikTok waives it for new sellers (first product listed within the last 90 days) for the following month, and existing sellers get their first 50 orders each month free. Tick 'Order Processing Fee exempt' in the calculator for those orders.

How is the TikTok Shop Philippines commission calculated?

Commission = (item price − seller discount) × the sub-category rate. Shipping and TikTok platform discounts are excluded. New sellers get 0% commission for their first 90 days.

Is the Shipping Service Fee charged on every order?

TikTok's seller fee overview says it is charged to all sellers on successfully delivered orders: 5.5% for Marketplace and 5% for Mall, capped at ₱100 per item, and not refunded if the order is later returned.

Are TikTok Shop PH fees VAT inclusive?

Yes. TikTok publishes all of these Philippine fee rates inclusive of VAT, and the calculator uses them as published.
