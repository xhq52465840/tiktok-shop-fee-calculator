FAQ

Which TikTok Shop fee changes happened in 2026?

Malaysia added a RM0.54 Platform Support Fee (15 Feb), raised the PayLater transaction fee to 4.86% (16 Oct), raised the BXP campaign-day fee to 7.02% (7 Oct) and revises commission rates from 31 Oct 2026. The Philippines added a 1%–1.5% Seller Growth Fee (from 27 Apr, as reported) and raised the Order Processing Fee from ₱3 to ₱5 on 1 Sep 2026.
